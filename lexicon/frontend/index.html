      Search type

      All query types work together. Pick one above to search that way only.

      Query syntax
      moon tide
      Documents with both words. AND is the default.
      moon OR monsoon
      Documents with either word.
      moon NOT tide
      Documents with moon but not tide. -tide works too.
      "inverted index"
      The exact phrase, words side by side and in order.
      (a OR b) AND c
      Parentheses group. NOT binds tightest, then AND, then OR.
      mon* *tion h*o
      Star matches any run of letters, at the start, middle or end.
      c?ral
      Question mark matches exactly one letter.

      Search type buttons. With none selected, every kind of query above works at once. Boolean reads only AND, OR, NOT and parentheses. Phrase treats everything you type as one exact phrase. Wildcard reads each word as a pattern. Soundex finds words that sound alike, so monsoom finds monsoon. Spell check compares your words with the index and offers corrections.

      Case, accents and possessives are ignored: CAFÉ finds cafe, and Moon's finds moon.
